Terms of Service & Voluntary Guidelines

Simple guidelines, voluntary donor ethics, and safety standards for the BloodRadar network.

Welcome to BloodRadar. By downloading, accessing, or using the BloodRadar mobile application or website ("Platform"), you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree with these Terms, you must not use the Platform.

100% Voluntary, Unpaid & Non-Commercial Network

BloodRadar is an emergency communication platform created solely to facilitate altruistic, unpaid blood and platelet donation between willing voluntary donors and patient families in medical need. Any commercial use or monetization of blood or platelets is strictly prohibited.

1. Strict Prohibition of Commercial Transactions

In accordance with the Supreme Court of India ruling (1996), the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, and the National AIDS Control Organisation (NACO) statutory framework:

  • Commercial Blood & Platelet Trading is Illegal: Buying, selling, trading, or demanding monetary compensation, gifts, or financial incentives in exchange for blood, platelets, or blood components is strictly forbidden.
  • Immediate Account Ban & Prosecution: Any user, whether requester or donor, who attempts to solicit money or commercial exchange via BloodRadar will face permanent account termination and immediate reporting to law enforcement authorities.
  • Zero Commission: BloodRadar charges zero platform fees, commissions, or brokerage for connecting donors with patients.

2. Voluntary Donor Health Declaration

When registering as a voluntary donor on BloodRadar, you affirm and represent that:

  • You are between 18 and 65 years of age.
  • You weigh a minimum of 45 kg (50 kg for Single Donor Platelet apheresis).
  • You are in good general health, with no active symptoms of infectious diseases (including HIV, Hepatitis B/C, Syphilis, or Malaria).
  • You have completed the statutory recovery cooldown interval under NBTC guidelines: 90 days for Men, 120 days for Women (Whole Blood), and a 28-day reference window for platelet (SDP) apheresis following whole blood donation.
  • You are not currently under medical disqualifications for blood or platelet donation (e.g., recent major surgery, pregnancy, or specific prescription medications).

3. Emergency Requester Obligations & Code of Conduct

Users issuing emergency blood or platelet requests agree to:

  • Issue requests exclusively for genuine, active medical emergencies with verified patient hospitalization.
  • Provide accurate hospital location details, patient blood requirements, and point-of-contact information.
  • Immediately cancel or mark the request as "Fulfilled" in the app once required blood units are secured to prevent unnecessary donor dispatch.
  • Treat all voluntary donors with utmost respect, courtesy, and gratitude.

4. Hospital Blood Bank Role & Medical Disclaimer

BloodRadar is a technology platform that routes real-time communications. BloodRadar is NOT a blood bank, hospital, clinic, or medical service provider:

  • Clinical Screening Required: All blood collections, donor screening, medical suitability checks, cross-matching, component separation, and transfusion procedures must take place exclusively at licensed blood centres and hospital transfusion medicine departments in compliance with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and NBTC guidelines.
  • Mandatory Cross-Match: Patient families and medical personnel are solely responsible for conducting mandatory compatibility cross-matching and laboratory testing before any transfusion occurs.
  • No Medical Liability: BloodRadar is not responsible or liable for any medical complications, infectious transmissions, misdiagnoses, delays in donor arrival, or hospital transfusion outcomes.

5. Location Services & 10km Broadcast Usage

BloodRadar utilizes on-demand location data to calculate 10km proximity geofencing. By requesting emergency assistance or enabling donor alerts, you consent to proximity calculation relative to the designated hospital location.

6. Account Termination & Enforcement

BloodRadar reserves the right to suspend, disable, or permanently delete any user account without prior notice if:

  • The user is found to be engaged in commercial blood solicitation or financial fraud.
  • The user broadcasts false, frivolous, or abusive emergency alerts.
  • The user violates our Zero-Trust Privacy rules by abusing donor/requester contact details.

7. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These Terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with the Platform shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

STATUTORY COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY NOTICE

BloodRadar is a voluntary technology facilitator for emergency blood and platelet communication. BloodRadar does not collect, store, buy, sell, or transport blood, platelets, or human tissue. All blood donation, clinical screening, compatibility testing, component separation, and transfusion are conducted exclusively by licensed blood centres and hospital transfusion medicine departments in compliance with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, and National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) guidelines. Commercial blood banking and paid donations are strictly prohibited by law.
